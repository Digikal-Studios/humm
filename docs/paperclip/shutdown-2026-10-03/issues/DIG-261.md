### Document `decision-policy`: Delegated board decisions (DIG-261)

Revision 1 (id `68063c42-90a7-4edf-ae99-67b0a8d5c13e`), updated 2026-09-30T10:12:34.043Z by CEO.

#### Delegated board decisions (board decision DIG-261, 2026-09-30)

Requested by the founder on DIG-261, 2026-09-30: go through every task waiting on a board decision, have DevGuru review each one with full context, apply the best decision automatically, and automate as much of this as possible. The founder grants DevGuru that authority. Applied the same day to all nine agent briefs through the Paperclip instructions API.

##### What DevGuru decides

DevGuru holds the founder's delegated authority to decide, and to apply, any question an agent would otherwise escalate to the board, within the guardrails below:

- Technical and engineering choices: approach, scope, how an acceptance criterion is read, which of two designs, whether a finding is required or a suggestion.
- Task posture: unblock an issue whose blockers are gone, reassign, re-scope, split, or cancel obsolete and duplicate work.
- Merge decisions on pull requests after DevGuru's adversarial review (DIG-250). TechLead executes the merge.
- Answers to `ask_user_questions` and `request_confirmation` cards other agents raise on technical matters.

##### Still founder-only

- New or recurring spend; hiring; credentials, secrets and access grants; releases and anything published to players or third parties; deleting or migrating production data; pausing or resuming agents; product or game-design direction that the Game & Experience Designer has marked as a founder call; anything irreversible without a documented rollback.
- For these DevGuru saves one `ask_user_questions` card with `resolverPolicy: "human_only"`, a recommendation and a default, and sets its task `in_review`.

##### How DevGuru applies a decision

1. Read the whole context first: the issue, its documents, the full thread, related issues, and the branch or pull request on repo.digikal.com. A decision made from the title alone is a finding against DevGuru.
2. Write the decision as a comment on the affected issue starting `DevGuru decision (DIG-261):` with what was decided, why, the alternatives rejected, and what is now expected of whom.
3. Apply it in the same run: `PATCH` status, assignee or `blockedByIssueIds`; `accept`, `reject` or `respond` to the card; create child issues where work follows. Never leave a decision as prose only.
4. Runs are capped at 20 cross-issue writes. Decide in priority order; when the cap is near, put the remaining items in one child issue assigned to yourself so a fresh run continues, and end the current run with a disposition.
5. Record every decision in a `decisions` document on the task that carried the backlog, one row per issue: decision, rationale, write applied.

##### How other agents route decisions

- A technical question that would previously have gone to the founder or CEO is raised as an `ask_user_questions` or `request_confirmation` card on the author's task with `addresseeAgentId` set to DevGuru (`3b54867a-f9b0-42bc-8ec9-94a41d8f7b5b`) and no `resolverPolicy`, stating the recommended option. The task goes `in_review` and DevGuru's answer is acted on as a founder decision.
- Founder-only matters keep `resolverPolicy: "human_only"`.
- CEO's hourly sweep routes any issue found waiting on a board decision (blocked with no live blocker, stale technical cards, unreviewed pull requests) to DevGuru as one courier task, and no longer asks the founder for technical decisions.

##### Section added to every author brief (inside "## Review policy")

- **Decisions (board decision DIG-261, 2026-09-30).** DevGuru holds the founder's delegated authority for technical decisions. When you need a decision you would previously have asked the founder or CEO for (approach, scope, how an acceptance criterion is read, whether a blocker still applies, which option to build), raise it as an `ask_user_questions` or `request_confirmation` card on your task with `addresseeAgentId` set to DevGuru (`3b54867a-f9b0-42bc-8ec9-94a41d8f7b5b`) and no `resolverPolicy`, state your recommended option, set the task `in_review`, and act on DevGuru's answer as a founder decision. Only spend, hiring, credentials and secrets, releases and publishing, production data deletion, and product direction the Game & Experience Designer marks as a founder call still go to the founder with `resolverPolicy: "human_only"`.
